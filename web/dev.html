🐢 TamaPoke

LOCAL TEST flash: 1.36.0-gen3-local · 25 Aug 2026

A 386-Pokémon local test build for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75. Install it from your browser — no ZIP download, no release files, no Arduino, no manual firmware files.

⚠️ REQUIRED: flashing alone is not enough.
After step 1, you must complete step 2 and copy the Gen-3 sprites to the microSD. For a new, empty or uncertain card, choose Load full 386 package. Use the smaller Gen-3 update only if #1–251 are already complete on that card.

⚠️ Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1Flash the firmware

Plug the board in via USB and press install. Pick the port and wait.

Installs firmware 1.36.0-gen3-local from 25 Aug 2026. The same version is shown on the board in Settings after flashing.

Browser not supported (use Chrome/Edge). Needs HTTPS.

🔄 Updating to a new version? Install without erasing to keep your Pokémon — updates flash only the firmware parts and leave the save area untouched. Only a full Erase wipes your saved pet. First time on a fresh board? You can erase, no problem.

2Load the sprites onto the microSD

Mandatory after every first Gen-3 firmware installation: the firmware does not contain the Gen-3 sprite files itself.

Put a microSD into the board (it formats itself). Press Connect board and then Load sprites: they download and copy over USB automatically (~Gen-3 update package, a few minutes). Don't remove the card.

Close the step-1 install tab first (only one program can use the port at a time).

Have your own .bin files?

The Gen-3 update assumes #1–251 are already on the card. Use the full package for an empty/new card.

🎨 Want to make your own sprites? See how to generate & load them in the repo.

How to play

  
Care for your egg until it hatches, feed it, play, train it and complete them
     all. Tap the creature to pet it; swipe to open the Pokédex (sideways), the
     stat card (up) or set the clock (down); the physical PWR button turns the
     screen off (short press) or everything off (long press).
     More in the repository.
